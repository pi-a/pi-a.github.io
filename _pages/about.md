Through close collaboration with industry partners and multidisciplinary research teams, I have developed a strong understanding of real-world business needs and technical constraints. My background in data-driven methods and operational optimization, including building data pipelines and ETL workflows, conducting statistical analyses, and developing predictive models, enables me to design solutions in a structured, end-to-end manner. I am particularly experienced in translating complex datasets into clear, actionable insights, for example by deploying interactive analytics dashboards and applying machine learning–based feature extraction to support and improve decision-making processes.
